Safety first
Working safe, every job
🦺

Safety is a condition of employment — and everyone's responsibility.

Every Envision employee, subcontractor, and supervisor follows our safe work practices. Wear your required PPE, inspect your equipment before use, report unsafe conditions immediately, and stop work if a condition is unsafe. Supervisors run daily jobsite inspections and toolbox talks. By following these procedures we keep our crews, customers, and the public safe.

🪜
Fall protection
Required at 6 ft or higher. Inspect harnesses; 100% tie-off where required.
🥽
PPE
Hard hats, eye & hearing protection, gloves, respirators, and work boots per jobsite.
🏠
Roofing
Watch the weather continuously and stop work in unsafe conditions.
🎨
Painting
Ensure ventilation and wear respiratory protection around chemicals & fumes.
⚡
Electrical
Inspect tools & cords before use. GFCIs required on all jobsites.
💧
Heat illness
Drink water, take breaks, and know the signs of heat exhaustion & stroke.
🧯
Fire & emergency
Keep extinguishers accessible; know evacuation routes & emergency contacts.
📋
Report it
Report every injury, near miss, and unsafe condition to your supervisor right away.
Full details are in the Envision Painting & Roofing Safety Manual — ask your supervisor or check Resources for a copy.
